Support

Help with the apps, licence keys and purchases.

Contact

Open a request on the RealZ support page (a free GitHub account is needed to post). Describe what happened, which app and version you use, and your Windows version. Please don't post your licence key or order details publicly.

Purchases, receipts and refunds

Purchases are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller. Your receipt email from Lemon Squeezy lets you manage your order, see your licence key and contact us privately about it. Refunds are covered in the refund policy.

Lost licence key

Your key is in your Lemon Squeezy receipt email and in your Lemon Squeezy order page. If you can't find it, reply to the receipt email and we'll resend it.
