Support

Help with the apps, licence keys and purchases.

Contact

Open a request on the RealZ support page (a free GitHub account is needed to post). Describe what happened, which app and version you use, and your Windows version. Please don't post your licence key or order details publicly.

Purchases, receipts and refunds

Purchases are paid through PayPal. PayPal sends your receipt, and we email your licence key to your PayPal email address within 24 hours. Reply to that email to contact us privately about your order. Refunds are covered in the refund policy.

Lost licence key

Your key is in the email we sent after your purchase (check spam too). If you can't find it, email avariofrancis99@gmail.com from your PayPal email address and we'll resend it.
