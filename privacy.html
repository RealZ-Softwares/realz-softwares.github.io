Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

RealZ is run by an independent software developer in Jamaica. This policy covers this website, the RealZ apps (Media Forge, NOVA, Fetch) and purchases.

The apps

The apps run on your computer and do not collect, send or sell any personal data. They contain no telemetry, analytics or advertising. Your files never leave your PC unless you choose to download or share something yourself. When you activate a Pro licence key, the app sends only that key and an anonymous PC label (not your PC or user name) to Lemon Squeezy to check it; after that Pro works offline, with a quick re-check about once a week when you're online.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record and processes your payment. Lemon Squeezy shares your name, email address, country and order details with us so we can deliver your licence and support you. We never see or store your card details. See Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy.

We use your order information only to provide your licence, support and refunds, and we don't sell or share it with anyone else. Ask us to delete it at any time through the support page, unless we must keep it for tax or legal reasons.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
