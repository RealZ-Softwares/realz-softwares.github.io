Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

RealZ is run by an independent software developer in Jamaica. This policy covers this website, the RealZ apps (RealZ Forge, RealZ NOVA, RealZ Fetch) and purchases.

The apps

The apps run on your computer and do not collect, send or sell any personal data. They contain no telemetry, analytics or advertising. Your files never leave your PC unless you choose to download or share something yourself. Pro licence keys are checked on your PC; nothing is sent anywhere when you activate one.

RealZ NOVA contacts nothing unless you turn on an online feature. If you add your own OpenSubtitles API key and search for subtitles, RealZ NOVA sends that key and the file name to OpenSubtitles.com under their terms.

FF suite (FFmpeg). When you choose to install it, the app downloads the official build from its publisher's GitHub page and checks it against the published checksum. No personal data is sent.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Purchases

Payments are processed by PayPal. PayPal shares your name, email address and order details with us so we can send your licence key and support you. We never see or store your card details. See PayPal's privacy statement.

We use your order information only to provide your licence, support and refunds, and we don't sell or share it with anyone else. Ask us to delete it at any time through the support page, unless we must keep it for tax or legal reasons.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
