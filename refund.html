Refund Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

If a RealZ Pro upgrade doesn't work for you, you can get a full refund within 14 days of purchase. Tell us briefly what went wrong; we may offer a fix first, but the choice is yours.

How to ask

Reply to the email your licence key came in. Refunds go back to your PayPal account or card through PayPal. How long it takes to show depends on your bank or card.

After a refund

Please remove the licence key from your PCs (in the app: Pro → Remove key from this PC); the app returns to its free version. Your files and workflows are not affected.

After 14 days

Refunds after 14 days are at our discretion, for example when a problem can't be fixed. Your local consumer rights still apply.
