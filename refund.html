Refund Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

If a RealZ Pro upgrade doesn't work for you, you can get a full refund within 14 days of purchase. Tell us briefly what went wrong; we may offer a fix first, but the choice is yours.

Reply to your Lemon Squeezy receipt email, or use your Lemon Squeezy order page. Refunds go back to the original payment method through Lemon Squeezy. How long it takes to show depends on your bank or card.

After a refund

After 14 days

Refunds after 14 days are at our discretion, for example when a problem can't be fixed. Your local consumer rights still apply.
