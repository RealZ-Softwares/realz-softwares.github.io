Is Pro a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep Pro, including free updates for Media Forge 1.x.

How many PCs can I use it on?

Your Pro licence is for you, on up to 3 of your own computers. Moving to a new PC? Deactivate the old one inside Media Forge to free its slot.

Does it upload my files?

Never. Everything runs on your PC and it works offline. There is no telemetry. Activating Pro checks your key with Lemon Squeezy, then Pro keeps working offline.
