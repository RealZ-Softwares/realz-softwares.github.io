A few things you might want to know

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase.

How many PCs can I use it on?

Your Pro license covers up to three PCs.

Does RealZ Forge upload my files?

Your media is processed on your PC. You don't need to upload it to a conversion service.

What do I need to run it?

A PC running 64-bit Windows 10 or Windows 11, plus enough free space for your converted files.

Why does Windows show "unknown publisher"?

RealZ Forge isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show an unknown-publisher or SmartScreen warning. Download it using the official link on this page and check the file's source before running it.

Where's FFmpeg?

RealZ Forge uses FFmpeg to process your media. It's installed separately, and RealZ Forge will guide you through getting it set up.
